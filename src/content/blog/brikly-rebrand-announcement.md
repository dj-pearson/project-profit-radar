## The short version

We have rebranded to **Brikly**. Our product, our team, our pricing, your data,
and your logins are all unchanged. The only thing that is different is the name
on the door and the domain you visit — **[brikly.net](https://brikly.net)**.

If you are a current customer: **you do not need to do anything**. Your account,
subscription, integrations, and data carry over automatically.

---

## Why we rebranded

A trademark conflict surfaced around our previous name. Rather than fight a
prolonged legal battle that would distract us from building great construction
software, we made the decision to rebrand cleanly and move forward under a new
name we love: **Brikly**.

"Brikly" captures what we are: the foundational software small and mid-size
contractors use to stack wins on top of wins. Simple. Sturdy. Built to last.

- **Your data.** Every project, estimate, time entry, invoice, photo, and report
  is exactly where you left it.
- **Your integrations.** QuickBooks sync, Google Calendar, Outlook, Stripe, and
  every other connected service continue to work without reconfiguration.
- **Your team's logins.** Same email, same password, same SSO configuration.
- **Your support team.** The humans you know are still answering your tickets.
- **Our roadmap.** Every feature we were building is still being built.
- **Our commitment to SMB contractors.** We still exist for one reason — to give
  small and mid-size construction companies the real-time financial visibility
  that the Procores of the world charge enterprise prices for.

## For customers: what (if anything) to do

**If you have nothing unusual set up**: nothing. Just visit
[brikly.net](https://brikly.net) next time.

**If you have allowlists** (firewalls, email filters, security software):

- Allow `*.brikly.net` in addition to the old domain.
- Allow `support@brikly.net`, `noreply@brikly.net`, `billing@brikly.net`.

**If you have saved API keys or webhooks**:

- The API is now served from `https://api.brikly.net`. Old endpoints continue to
  serve 200 responses until **September 30, 2026**, but we recommend updating
  integrations at your convenience before then.
- Webhook payloads are unchanged except for the `source` field which now reads
  `"brikly"`.

**If you have documentation referencing us**: feel free to update to "Brikly",
but it's not urgent — redirects will keep everything working.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this a different company?

No. Same company, same team, same ownership, same office. Only the name on the
sign is different.

### Will I be charged anything because of the rebrand?

No. Your billing is untouched. Same card on file, same monthly charge, same
invoice schedule.

### Do I need to reinstall the mobile app?

Your existing install will be updated in place on the next app update. The new
version is branded Brikly but otherwise unchanged.

### What happens to my email notifications?

Email notifications will come from `@brikly.net` addresses starting today.
Please allowlist these senders: `noreply@brikly.net`, `support@brikly.net`,
`billing@brikly.net`.

### I signed a contract under the old name. Is it still valid?

Yes. Contracts, MSAs, and data processing agreements executed under the previous
name remain fully in force. If your legal team needs a formal name-change
addendum, email [support@brikly.net](mailto:support@brikly.net) and we will send
one.
